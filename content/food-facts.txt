ENTRY 01
TITLE: Guava vs Many Common Fruits
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Guava is particularly notable for its vitamin C content and also provides fiber. The exact nutritional amount depends on fruit size and variety.
TAKEAWAY: Guava can be a nutrient-dense fruit choice, not just a snack.

ENTRY 02
TITLE: Whole Fruit vs Fruit Juice
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Whole fruit retains its natural structure and fiber, while juice is easier to consume quickly and usually provides less fiber per equivalent amount of fruit.
TAKEAWAY: Choose whole fruit more often when you want more fiber and chewing.

ENTRY 03
TITLE: Orange vs Guava
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Both can provide vitamin C, but guava is often especially rich in vitamin C while also providing useful fiber.
TAKEAWAY: You do not need expensive exotic fruits to get vitamin C.

ENTRY 04
TITLE: Banana vs Orange
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Bananas are notable for potassium and carbohydrates, while oranges provide vitamin C, hydration and a different micronutrient profile. Both can fit into a balanced diet.
TAKEAWAY: Choose based on your meal, preference and nutritional needs rather than declaring one universally superior.

ENTRY 05
TITLE: Papaya As A Fruit Choice
TYPE: FOOD FACT
CONTENT: Papaya provides vitamin C, carotenoids and other nutrients while being relatively easy to include as a snack or breakfast fruit.
TAKEAWAY: Variety matters more than searching for one perfect fruit.

ENTRY 06
TITLE: Amla And Vitamin C
TYPE: FOOD FACT
CONTENT: Amla is known for being rich in vitamin C, although the exact amount varies with preparation and processing.

ENTRY 07
TITLE: Watermelon And Hydration
TYPE: FOOD FACT
CONTENT: Watermelon contains a high proportion of water and can contribute to fluid intake while also providing carbohydrates and micronutrients.
TAKEAWAY: Water-rich fruits can complement, but not completely replace, normal fluid intake.

ENTRY 08
TITLE: Whole Fruit vs Fruit Juice
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Whole fresh fruit provides fiber and requires chewing, while fruit juice is easier to consume quickly and usually contains much less fiber.
TAKEAWAY: Whole fruit is generally more filling than fruit juice.

ENTRY 09
TITLE: Curd vs Milk
TYPE: COMPARISON

ENTRY 10
TITLE: Paneer vs Tofu
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Paneer provides protein and calcium but can also be relatively high in fat depending on preparation. Tofu provides plant-based protein and its nutrient profile depends on how it is made.
TAKEAWAY: Compare the full nutritional profile and serving size rather than choosing based on the label alone.

ENTRY 11
TITLE: Eggs vs Paneer
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Eggs provide protein and several micronutrients, while paneer provides protein and calcium. Their calorie and fat contents vary with portion and preparation.

ENTRY 12
TITLE: Eggs vs Dal
TYPE: COMPARISON
CONTENT: Eggs provide complete animal protein along with several micronutrients, while dal provides plant protein, carbohydrates, fiber and minerals.
